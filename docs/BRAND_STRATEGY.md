# Soriko Club — brand strategy

## Positioning

Soriko is not positioned as a generic Pokémon catalogue.

It combines:

1. Commerce — authentic product, strong selection and European fulfilment.
2. Japan — curated access to Japanese sealed product and exclusives.
3. Culture — history, sets, illustration, collecting and preservation.
4. Community — alerts, newsletter, wishlist and collector identity.

## Core customer journeys

### Open
For customers who enjoy opening current sets.

### Keep
For sealed collectors who care about condition, preservation and presentation.

### Japan
For collectors looking for Japanese boxes, exclusives and releases difficult to source in Europe.

### Complete
For collectors pursuing specific sets, products or wishlist items.

## Content system

### Soriko Chronicle
Weekly or biweekly newsletter.

Recurring structure:
- NOW — current release / market development
- THEN — one moment from Pokémon TCG history
- JAPAN — one Japanese product, release or collecting story
- THE DROP — restocks / upcoming Soriko inventory
- THE PIECE — one noteworthy product or card

### Soriko Journal
Evergreen editorial archive:
- history
- collecting guides
- Japanese product
- preservation
- set stories
- artist / illustration context
- buyer guides

## Community roadmap

Phase 1:
- email list
- wishlist / stock alerts
- editorial content
- early access to drops

Phase 2:
- Collector Profile
- collection goals
- community discussions or Discord integration
- loyalty / club benefits

Phase 3:
- collection tracking
- trading / meetups if operationally viable
- Soriko events and collaborations

## Soriko Lab

3D printed accessories should focus on generic collector utility:
- booster box display stands
- slab stands
- card display risers
- storage dividers
- deck / binder organisation
- wall or shelf display systems

Avoid selling unlicensed reproductions of Pokémon characters, logos or other protected artwork without appropriate permission.

## Design direction

Editorial, collector-first, Japanese-influenced but not themed as a novelty anime shop.

Palette:
- warm paper
- ink black
- anniversary yellow
- Japan red
- electric blue

Typography:
- strong grotesk / sans for commerce
- serif accents for archive/history

The visual system must remain recognisably Soriko even when official Pokémon product imagery is removed.
